72% of students achieved our 2x growth target during the school period. Median growth was 2.75x. This demonstrates that our curriculum and ClearFluency implementation IS working when students are actively engaged.

4 Key Actions for Spring-to-Spring 2x

For Spring 2026 (immediate actions):

1

Re-test Students Who Mastered via TTS

Problem: "Aligned" students (ORF = HMG+1) have 34% severe underperformance rate. Some may have inflated HMG from passing tests by listening (TTS) rather than reading. Solution: Re-assess comprehension without TTS for low-growth students to identify true skill level.

2

Enforce Daily Reading Time

Problem: 48% of students not meeting time expectations. Solution: Alert system for students falling behind, campus accountability for time-on-task metrics.

3

Investigate Nova Academy Austin

Problem: F-W growth of only 1.10x vs 2.75x network median - severe outlier. Solution: Immediate deep dive on curriculum implementation, staffing, ClearFluency usage.

For Next Year's W-W Results:

4

Summer Reading Maintenance Program

Problem: 17% of students lost >10 RIT points over summer. These students have only 1.10x W-W growth. Solution: Mandatory minimum app usage, weekly check-ins during summer break. (Won't help Spring 2026, but will improve next year's W-W results.)

Bottom Line

Our curriculum works during the school year (72% meeting 2x during F-W). The W-W gap is largely due to summer learning loss. For Spring 2026, we need to: (1) re-test students who may have inflated HMG from TTS, (2) ensure time on task, and (3) fix Nova Austin. Summer reading maintenance will help next year's W-W results.
