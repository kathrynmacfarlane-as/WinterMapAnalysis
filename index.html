Analysis: Students Who Spent >75% of Year in Early Years Reading

Methodology

We analyzed reading test results to identify G3+ students who spent significant time in early years reading this year:

  • Students who passed Grade 2 placement test went straight to G3+ (0% EY time)
  • For other students, we calculated time from school start (Aug 13) or first placement test to Grade 2 mastery
  • Students with 3+ early years tests who never passed G2 are considered "still in EY" (100% time)
Key Finding: Minimal Impact on Results

The Rostered Grade Classification Already Captures Most High-EY Students

Of the G3+ students who spent >75% of their school year in early years reading:

  • 125 students total spent >75% of year in early years
  • 108 (86%) are already classified as Early Lit by the existing flag
  • Only 1 additional student with meaningful engagement (3+ tests) gets added
  • This student has no MAP growth data, so there's no impact on results

Why the Overlap Is So High

The rostered grade classification already captures most students who spent significant time in early years because:

  • HMG reflects recent mastery - Students with HMG 0-2 are still working through early lit
  • Early Lit flag is accurate - 86% of high-EY-time students already have Early Lit = YES
  • Recently advanced students - Students who just mastered G2 have high HMG (3+) but spent most of their time in EY

The only gap is students who:

  1. Recently mastered G2 (so HMG > 2)
  2. Don't have a current rostered grade set
  3. Have Early Lit = NO flag

This gap is very small (6 students total with >50% EY time not captured) and most lack MAP data.
