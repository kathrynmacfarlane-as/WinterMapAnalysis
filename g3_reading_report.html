W-W Growth: Strong Performance

53% Met 2x W-W Target - Significantly Better Than Early Years

W-W performance is notably strong in G3+ Reading compared to Early Years (17% rostered K-2):

  • Median W-W growth: 2.00x
  • 31 of 59 students with W-W data met the 2x target
  • Top performers: Alpha Miami at 3.25x median W-W (75% meeting target) and Alpha Brownsville at 2.50x median (62% meeting target)
Campus Alert: Alpha High School

Alpha High School Shows Concerning W-W Pattern

While F-W performance is acceptable (2.50x median, 56% meeting target), the W-W results are alarming:

  • Median W-W growth: -2.00x (negative growth)
  • Only 22% met W-W target (2 of 9 students with W-W data)
  • 5 of 9 students with W-W data showed negative growth
  • 13 of 36 students (36%) have F-W growth below 1x

Investigation needed: Summer learning loss may be particularly severe for high school students, or there may be test-taking issues.
